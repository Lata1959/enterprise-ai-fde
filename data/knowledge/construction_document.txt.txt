CONSTRUCTION OPERATIONS KNOWLEDGE BASE

Equipment Management

Construction companies must maintain accurate
records of equipment deployed across project sites.

Every asset should have:
- Unique asset identification
- Current location
- Assigned project
- Responsible operator
- Maintenance history
- Inspection status

Preventive Maintenance

Equipment should undergo scheduled preventive
maintenance based on operating hours and manufacturer
recommendations.

Overdue maintenance should generate an alert
for the responsible site manager.

Project Management

Project managers should monitor:
- Project schedule
- Equipment utilisation
- Workforce availability
- Material availability
- Safety incidents

Equipment Utilisation

Low equipment utilisation can indicate poor
resource allocation.

Equipment utilisation should be evaluated using
operating hours divided by available working hours.

Safety

Equipment with expired safety inspections must
not be assigned to active construction operations.
